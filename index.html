What the study covers.

The workspace shows the rules and inputs behind each result. Use it for early feasibility and professional review.

Which sites can I study?

The screening model covers Bangkok’s R.8 district for condominium, apartment, and office concepts within the documented size and frontage limits. The Bang Son example includes mapped streets and building footprints. For other plots, confirm zoning and supply the relevant survey and road data.

What files can I bring in?

Import WGS84 GeoJSON polygons for plots and multi-parcel studies. The survey comparison also accepts a single polygon in WGS84 / UTM 47N. You can supply building footprints with a numeric height_m field for the context and sun study.

What can I take into other tools?

Use GeoJSON for GIS and study records, DXF for metric CAD linework, IFC4 for BIM massing, and GLB for 3D presentation geometry. The workspace explains what each export contains.

What still needs professional review?

Confirm official boundaries and zoning, road rights, fire access, utilities, parking, and applicable permit requirements with the project team. The built-in concepts are illustrative massing based on the modeled rules and supplied inputs.
